## 🧠 Overview

**Endacopia** is a surreal point-and-click adventure that disguises unsettling psychological horror beneath the appearance of an innocent educational game. Players guide a young boy named Mellow through bizarre environments where strange characters, hidden secrets, and disturbing mysteries gradually replace familiar reality. The experience blends nostalgic visuals with unpredictable storytelling and nonlinear exploration.

The game combines point-and-click exploration, environmental puzzles, psychological horror, first-person encounters, surreal storytelling, multiple endings, retro-inspired visuals, and atmospheric adventure into one unforgettable experience.

Designed for horror fans, indie gamers, adventure enthusiasts, puzzle lovers, streamers, retro game collectors, story-driven players, and Windows users.
